# Room Six 0.4.0: opening, guidance, claims and sound

This revision addresses the player's first-minute experience and the clarity of investigation, rather than claiming measured retention improvement.

## Story context

Leila owns the villa and plans to sell it. She invited the four other returning members of the old group to collect belongings and old documents before the sale, while hoping to clarify Raha's fate. The gathering is not a construction or renovation job. The listing floor plan is explicitly named «نقشه آگهی فروش ویلا»: a simplified room layout prepared for the sale advertisement, separate from the original building plan. The introduction, briefing, exhibit E01, its source, opening scene and Leila's new interview question agree on this purpose.

## First meaningful interaction

Immediately after entering the living room, the player reads an actual detail from E01: the listing plan has five visible rooms and an unexplained services area. Comparing the original earns the real E02 document early, delivered by Leila from the property's archive folder. Accepting Behzad's explanation instead adds a short source-verification question and the normal archive visit. A wrong source answer offers a specific correction and retry; no evidence is removed and no route is permanently locked. Existing saves resume without replaying the opening.

## Investigation route

The visible route is scene inspection → initial interviews → claims and evidence comparison → chronology → independent reports. Its explanation makes purposeful returns to a room or witness explicit. The next-step button points to a reachable document, question or deduction and highlights that action; it does not automatically jump between sections. The graph is tested through both opening choices to a complete report.

## Optional guides

All ten deductions and four confrontations now have three authored levels: where to start, what to compare, and the exact documents/action. Numeric and paper guides include the calculation or rotation only in the final requested level. The final level can place already-discovered recommended documents on the table; it never collects unavailable evidence or submits an answer. Missing documents have links to their accessible locations. Revealed levels persist across save/reload. Following a missing-document link also saves a bookmark back to the exact previous check, with its revealed guide levels intact. These guides remain opt-in; viewing a guide cannot complete an investigation by itself.

Each screen states the exact claim being tested and the type of information to compare. Missing evidence and unrelated selections produce different, usable feedback. Correct names without the full final evidence chain still fail the report.

## Encouragement

Brief, case-specific responses follow first clues, selected evidence/interview milestones, each new deduction/confrontation and chronology. Responses name what has become clearer without declaring an early culprit. Reward keys persist and revisiting the same record cannot farm new rewards. When a document dialog is open, the small message appears after it closes so it is actually visible.

## Sound

Room Six now has an identifiable repeating 16-step melody at a 0.76-second pulse (about 79 beats per minute and a 12-second phrase), replacing the very sparse long score. Quiet procedural leaf/wind ambience and occasional soft bird calls replace the rain. Existing sound/volume controls, pause/resume and offline operation are retained. Case 47 keeps its existing sound profile. The composition and ambience use generated oscillator/noise signals rather than third-party recordings; no music or environmental sample is downloaded.
